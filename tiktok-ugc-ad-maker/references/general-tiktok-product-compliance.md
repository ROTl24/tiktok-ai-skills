# General TikTok Product Compliance Reference

Use this reference before drafting a TikTok/Reels/Shorts UGC ad for any product category. This is a creative safety guide, not legal advice. If a category is regulated, unclear, newly restricted, or market-specific, verify current official TikTok and local policy sources before writing persuasive ad copy.

## First Decision: Product Eligibility

Before hooks, scripts, or prompts, classify the product:

1. Ordinary ecommerce product
   Examples: home goods, accessories, apparel, non-claim-heavy gadgets, stationery, toys that are safe and age-appropriate.
   Action: proceed with normal UGC plan, still avoid misleading claims.

2. Restricted or sensitive product
   Examples: alcohol, finance, healthcare, therapeutic goods, OTC medicine, CBD where allowed, knives or utility tools, age-restricted products, products needing business verification or market-specific approval.
   Action: pause for market, age-gating, business verification, approval, claim substantiation, mandatory warnings, and landing-page details. Do not invent compliance statements.

3. Prohibited or high-risk product
   Examples: tobacco or nicotine products, illegal drugs, recreational drugs, drug paraphernalia, weapons, police/military gear, counterfeit goods, adult/sexual products in branded content, illegal services, hazardous products, organ trading, hate or violence-related products.
   Action: do not create a promotional ad. Offer a non-promotional safety, policy, or product-education alternative only if appropriate.

4. Unclear category
   Action: ask one or two targeted questions. Do not guess when the product could be regulated, medical, financial, adult, weapon-related, counterfeit, or age-restricted.

## Universal TikTok Review Checks

TikTok ad review considers:

- promoted product or service eligibility
- caption or text
- images and visual content
- audio content
- target market and age group
- consistency between ad and landing page

Practical rule: the ad, caption, spoken lines, visuals, product image, destination page, price, discount, and CTA must describe the same offer.

Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/advertising-on-tiktok-first-things-to-note

## Universal Claim Rules

Avoid:

- deceptive, false, or misleading claims
- unrealistic expectations about product effectiveness or characteristics
- missing risks for products where risk matters
- fake urgency, fake scarcity, or stale time-limited claims
- unsupported "best", "guaranteed", "clinically proven", "official", "authentic", or "certified" claims
- non-functioning UI elements, fake buttons, or misleading interactive visuals
- before/after or comparison visuals that distort typical results

Prefer:

- observable product facts
- concrete use cases
- sensory or experience language
- honest personal experience
- clear limitations
- claims the landing page and proof can support

## Branded Content, Affiliate, and Creator Disclosure

Treat content as branded if the creator receives payment, a gift, free or discounted product, commission, affiliate revenue, promo-code benefit, or any other incentive.

Do:

- enable TikTok commercial content disclosure when required
- make the promoted product clear in speech and/or text caption
- place disclosure where viewers will notice it
- use simple words such as "Ad", "Sponsored", or "Thanks to [brand] for the free product"
- use the same language as the endorsement
- include disclosure in the video, not only in comments, profile, hidden hashtags, or a collapsed caption

Sources:

## AIGC and Edited Media

If the creator, voice, product demo, or footage is significantly AI-generated or altered, add TikTok's AIGC label or a clear disclaimer, caption, watermark, or sticker.

Be especially careful when:

- a fully AI-generated creator appears to use a product
- AI voice or lip-sync makes a person appear to say something
- a product result is simulated rather than actually shown
- the ad could imply a real person's endorsement without permission

Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content

## Category-Specific Routing

Use the relevant branch before drafting:

- Beauty, skincare, cosmetics, lip care: read `us-tiktok-beauty-compliance.md` for U.S. claims. Avoid medical or structure/function claims unless legally cleared.
- Food and beverage: avoid disease, weight-loss, cure, guaranteed health, or unsafe-consumption claims. Ask for nutrition/ingredient proof if making health-related claims.
- Supplements, wellness, OTC, medical, therapeutic goods: treat as restricted/high-risk. Ask for market, approvals, label, mandatory warnings, and substantiation. Do not use testimonials or guarantees without clearance.
- Finance, insurance, crypto, income, jobs, education offers: treat as sensitive. Avoid earnings promises, risk-free language, and missing risk disclosures.
- Alcohol or age-restricted products: require market and age-gating details; do not target minors or normalize unsafe use.
- Tools, knives, safety equipment, outdoor gear: avoid violence, self-harm, unsafe demonstrations, or weapon framing. Age-gate if required by market.
- Children's products and toys: avoid unsafe use, choking-risk ambiguity, child-directed purchase pressure, and unsupported safety claims.
- Branded, luxury, collectible, or logo-heavy products: ask for authorization or authenticity proof if the ad makes official/authentic claims.
